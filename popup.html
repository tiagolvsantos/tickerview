Keyboard help

Alt + Shift + T opens search (customize in chrome://extensions/shortcuts). Enter adds a symbol or opens its card. / focuses the watchlist filter; Esc clears it. Esc closes research or comparison. Research Previous/Next follows the filtered watchlist; ↑/↓ works on these controls and ticker buttons. Chart inspection uses ←/→. On a page, focus a cashtag and press Enter or Space.
